A FIXED STRONGLY REGULAR SUBGRAPH WITH MANY STRONGLY REGULAR COMPLETIONS
25 November 2026

There are more than 3.42417 × 10^110 switching classes on 496 vertices
outside the earlier Walsh half-frame family. Each gives ordinary graphs
with parameters (496,231,102,112), (496,240,120,112) and (495,238,109,119).
For one fixed labeled degree-240 graph, there are at least ceil(30^60/84)
pairwise switching-inequivalent parents at order 976. Each has an ordinary
degree-480 representative containing this very same labeled induced graph.
Complementing the induced graph and switching gives its degree-231 mate.

The row (496,231,102,112) is marked '?' in Brouwer's online table and
was already realized in srg496. The contribution here is the extension,
its multiplicity and a way to distinguish the resulting graphs. The
general Steiner equiangular-frame construction already allows arbitrary
Hadamard matrices at different points. The restriction to a regular
induced subgraph of this type and the proofs of inequivalence are essential.

1. The two frames

Use any of the three Steiner 2-(61,5,1) designs in
../starters. The marked point has 15 blocks
through it, each containing a part of four ordinary points. The other
168 blocks are the six symbol stars in each of 28 copies of T(6). Each
ordinary point occurs as a pair {a,b} in seven tiles. Index the frame
rows by the 183 blocks. Write chi_c(x)=(-1)^(c dot x) on F_2^3.

At the marked point install all 16 columns of a normalized real Hadamard
matrix of order 16 with its all-ones row deleted. The 15 remaining rows
are indexed by the parts. At each ordinary point install two groups of
eight columns. Their group-block coordinates are respectively +1 and -1.
For the tile with pair a<b the coordinates of the first group are
eps_t chi_c(x)(e_a-e_b), and those of the second are chi_d(x)(e_a+e_b).
Choose bijections from the seven flags to the seven nonzero characters,
independently for the two groups. In the first group follow the earlier
crossing-flag condition in ../PROOF.md. The three quiet
flags at each long point are a line in its Fano plane, and the product
of the four crossing signs oriented to the marked side is -1.
build.py gives an explicit choice in every example.

At an ordinary point the 15 rows are orthogonal and orthogonal to the
missing constant row: the two signed copies of a character on one flag
cancel between the two groups, distinct flags are orthogonal in both.
Each block contains five points and each local row has squared norm 16.
Thus FF^t=80I. Columns have norm squared 15, and distinct columns have
inner product ±1, from the truncated Hadamard in one fiber or the unique
common block between fibers. The full Seidel matrix S=F^t F-15I has
eigenvalues 65,-15.

Retain all 16 marked columns and the first eight at every ordinary point.
Call this submatrix B. On the group rows BB^t=48I. Each tile contributes
8 times the Laplacian of K6 on its six rows, with nonzero eigenvalue 48.
All cross terms vanish by character orthogonality. Thus its Seidel
eigenvalues are 33,-15. This calculation uses only orthogonality at the
marked point. Take z equal to 1 at the last group row and 1/2 at the two
marked symbols of every tile, with all other entries zero. The signs
B^t z are a Hadamard row on the marked fiber. On long fibers the four
crossing characters have product -1 after orientation and sum to ±2.
On short fibers the group coordinate gives ±1. Thus B^t z is a sign
eigenvector for eigenvalue 33, since B^t BB^t=48 B^t. Switching by
this word and taking negative entries gives the degree-231 graph.

The same kernel signing works simultaneously on the two frames. Use +1
on all marked columns. Within each part give two entire ordinary fibers
sign +1 and two sign -1, on each group of eight. The constant group sums
cancel and the nonconstant characters sum to zero. The signed full
Seidel matrix has row sum -15, as does its restriction. Its positive
entries give the degree-480 parent and the degree-240 induced graph.
Isolating and deleting a vertex in the negative Seidel orientation gives
the descendants (975,462,205,231) and (495,238,109,119). Taking the
same root in the 496-subgraph gives a literal induced 495-vertex
subgraph inside the 975-vertex descendant. Since each descendant
recovers its parent two-graph, the same fixed 495-vertex graph is
induced in at least ceil(30^60/84) inequivalent 975-vertex graphs.

2. Recognizing the new half-frames

Use four distinct rows of S-I whose componentwise product is constant.
This is invariant under switching and relabeling. The finite unsigned
starter checks in ../starters/recover.py continue to
exclude mixed quadruples. The slope test on an ordinary point is
unchanged. For three odd ordinary cells in one part plus a special point,
the product on the special fiber is a nonconstant Hadamard row. For four
odd cells in one part the old sign discrepancy persists. For two pairs
the special pair still has support a union of parts, and an ordinary
pair has one of 2,100 distinct supports, none of them a union of parts.
Consequently admissible quadruples occur only inside a cell. Each
ordinary cell gives 14. On the marked cell we read the four-column
relations of its normalized Hadamard matrix (sum zero in binary).

The five choices in hadamards.json have the following invariants in order.
The last column is the order of the permutation group on columns
preserving the fifteen unordered pairs of complementary half-blocks.

  class     four-sets    component sizes    group order
    0          140            16              322560
    1           76            16                9216
    2           44            16                1536
    3           28             8,8              2688
    4           28            16                2688

matrices.py obtains the matrices by closed-quadruple switches and
canonicalizes the pointed incidence structures. The equivalence and
pointed classes were also checked on every possible distinguished row
of McKay's five representatives by rectangles.py in the work directory reciprocal_rows_20261125 (not included).
The numbers of four-sets in the half-frames are 980,916,884,868,868.

In class 3, identify both special components even though they have size
eight. Normalize the internal sign matrix of each component D to -J.
This fixes switching on D up to a common sign. From any special vertex
the pattern on every other component D is constant in this normalization.
An ordinary vertex sees a nonconstant character on any ordinary fiber
outside its part. This identifies the two special components, which are
then merged. In all other cases the special component is the unique
16-cell. Its 15 projective row directions recover the parts. Taking the
special direction as reference on each ordinary cell recovers the seven
flags, the T(6) tiles, and their six stars by the earlier proof. Thus the
three designs can be distinguished from the abstract two-graph.

Count 15! assignments of the marked Hadamard rows to labeled parts,
6^56 × 30^4 ordinary flag structures and 2^364 flag sign choices. For a
given source, an isomorphism can induce at most 84 design permutations.
On the special fiber its switching is constant in the raw coordinates
(the internal sign matrix is -J), so its column permutation preserves
the 15 complementary row pairs. Each such permutation determines the
target row assignment. On ordinary fibers the prescribed flags fix the
linear action and leave at most 8 translations per fiber. Remaining
phase freedom is at most 2^(28-r) where r=22,19,19 on the three designs,
by the crossing parity argument of ../PROOF.md. For a
special group of order a this gives the lower bound

  [6^56 × 30^4 × 15! × 2^364 /(84 × a × 8^60)] × sum_designs 2^(r-28).

The four new weights relative to the old lower bound are 35,210,120,120.
They give 485 times the old bound in classes disjoint from the old
construction. The five types together give more than 3.43123 × 10^110.
The inequality applies separately to each of the three ordinary graph
parameter sets since a descendant determines its parent two-graph.

3. Many parents of exactly the same labeled graph

Fix the first design, marked matrix of class 4, and every first group.
At each ordinary point the second group can carry any of 7!/168=30 Fano
planes on its seven named flags. The first 496 columns are identical
for all 30^60 choices. To bound identification of the parents we recover
the choices from their full two-graphs.

First exclude four-sets supported on different point fibers. With two
odd fibers, evaluate on one of them. The contribution of the other is a
nonconstant row, while every even fiber contributes zero projective
direction. With four distinct points on a block, evaluate on one of
those four. With just three on a block use another point of that block
outside the four. With no three on a block, choose a further point of
the block of the first two away from the block of the last two. In the
latter two cases two distinct Hadamard directions survive. For a pair
of columns at p its row difference has support the 32 other design
points on eight of the fifteen blocks through p (constant on each point
fiber). Two such 32-sets cannot be complements in 61 points. Equality
for fibers p and q excludes the common block pq from either pencil.
Form the bipartite graph of the other 14 pencil blocks at p and q,
joining intersecting blocks. Equality requires the selected blocks to
be a union of components. recovery.py checks connectivity for every
one of the 1,830 pairs on each of the three designs. Hence the full
four-set hypergraph too is a disjoint union of local hypergraphs.

Each ordinary fiber contributes 14 planes in each half. A cross plane
contains two columns per half (the group row enforces even parity).
A pair difference in each half is one of the seven nonzero words of a
binary simplex code on the flags, each realized by four pairs. Thus if
the two simplex codes intersect in dimension d there are
28+16(2^d-1) four-sets. For d=0 these form two separate 8-components.
For d>0 the halves are connected and there are 44,76,140 four-sets.
The special fiber is the unique connected 16-cell with just 28
four-sets. This survives arbitrary local Fano choices.

Projective patterns on that special fiber recover the fifteen parts.
For each part, merge two 8-components precisely when their projective
directions on every component outside the part agree. For true twins
the joining block at each outside point is the same. For false twins
recovery.py finds 48 or 56 outside points at which different tile
flags occur, for each of the 90 same-part pairs. Distinct flags give
distinct directions even on a single half. With all 61 fibers
recovered, at any p the other fibers fall into 15 projective row
directions, giving its blocks of size five. This recovers the design.

For completeness the row through the special point splits an ordinary
16-cell into its two halves. First normalize switching on this whole
cell using its internal -J matrix. Each restriction of the remaining
14 rows gives seven distinct directions, in pairs, on either half.
Three directions multiply to a constant exactly on a Fano line. Hence
the two Fano structures on the paired flags are determined up to swapping
the two halves. A specified design permutation acts on the fourteen
pencil blocks and their pairs, so it prescribes the unordered pair of
Fano structures in the target. One member of that pair is the already
fixed first Fano plane. This determines the second one, even if the two
are equal. At most 84 labeled assignments are equivalent. Therefore
the number of parent two-graphs, and of ordinary degree-480 parents, is
at least ceil(30^60/84) > 5.04656 × 10^86. These parents have at most
28+60×140=8428 four-sets, fewer than the 61×140 local ones present in
any all-Walsh full Steiner (61,5) frame.

4. Explicit witnesses and reproduction

graphs/mixed_all_976_480.g6 uses each of the thirty second-half systems
twice. Its first 496 vertices induce graphs/mixed_all_496_240.g6.
graphs/mixed_all_496_231.g6 is its switched complement. The parent
contains 3052 four-sets and its design is recovered from graph6 alone.
The same first 496 graph is shared by d0_h4_976_480.g6.

Run matrices.py, build.py, recovery.py and audit.py in the srg environment.
audit.tsv lists the SHA-256 digest of each ordinary graph, the number of
pairs checked and switching invariants where applicable. audit.py uses
a separate graph6 decoder and checks degrees and all common-neighbor
counts for 75 files, along with all 15 induced-subgraph, switching and
descendant chains. Some of the 75 files deliberately encode the same
subgraph. Four full parents are subjected to recognition after further
switching and relabeling, with canonical design certificates checked
against the stored starters. recovery.out contains the exact integers.
