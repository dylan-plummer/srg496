# Extensions of the 496-vertex construction

The three supporting Steiner designs give more than \(7.06\times10^{107}\)
inequivalent regular two-graphs, each representing the degree-231 parameter
set that Brouwer lists with a question mark. The same counting lower
bound holds separately for the known degree-240 row and the known
495-vertex descendant row. [Proof](PROOF.md) ·
[Brouwer's 451–500 table](https://aeb.win.tue.nl/graphs/srg/srgtab451-500.html).

* [Three explicit Steiner designs, starters, and corresponding graphs](starters/).
  Designs 01 and 02 have incidence structures distinct from the original seed.
* [Catalogue and reproduction commands](CATALOGUE.md).
  On the original design, nine explicit two-graphs have full group order
  84. They yield 56 certified degree-231 graphs, 126 certified descendants,
  and 554,146,599,744 pairwise nonisomorphic degree-240 graphs by the
  specified family of switches.
* [All 56 degree-231 graph6 records](graphs/all_496_231.g6.gz) and
  [all 126 degree-238 descendant records](graphs/all_495_238.g6.gz).
  Decompress each file to obtain one graph6 record per line.
* [Verification output](audit.out). The certificates and automorphism
  orders are in [the 496 table](all_496_231_audit.tsv) and
  [the 495 table](all_495_238_audit.tsv).
* [Transfers](TRANSFERS.md). The 196 subframe approach and the mixed
  A6/E8 regular switch at 148 have arithmetic obstructions. A
  regular-action construction trial at 288 also terminated without a
  graph. The three parameter orders remain open on the routes discussed.
